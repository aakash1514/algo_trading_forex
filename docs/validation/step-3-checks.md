## Exact SMAs

The MA crossover now compares integer sums of bid+ask points. On these 2022 H1 runs no
exact tie occurs at a crossing, so the trades equal the step-2 float-SMA trades (table
above). A real tie that the float SMAs got wrong is in the EUR/USD 2022-01-03 M1 fixture
(SMA 5/20 at 12:47; see `ma-crossover.strategy.spec.ts`).

## Money: USD-quoted pairs at 0.1 lot

At 0.1 lot a USD-quoted pip is worth $1 (10,000 x 0.0001) for EUR/USD and $0.10 for
XAU/USD (10 oz x 0.01). The step-2 pip totals predict the USD totals exactly:
EUR/USD -272.2 pips -> -$272.20, XAU/USD -5,559.2 pips -> -$555.92; both verified runs
report those amounts.
